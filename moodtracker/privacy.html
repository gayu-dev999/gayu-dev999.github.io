MoodTracker 隱私權政策

本隱私權政策說明 Gayu Dev（以下稱「我們」） 在提供 MoodTracker 時如何處理資訊。

簡單來說： MoodTracker 的主要紀錄保存在您的裝置上。 Gayu Dev 沒有自己的伺服器、後端或資料庫來接收您的 MoodTracker 紀錄。 只有當您主動啟用 Google Calendar 整合時，指定的紀錄內容才會直接從您的裝置傳送至 Google。

02

儲存在您裝置上的資料

MoodTracker 使用裝置端儲存來保存您建立的紀錄。 目前版本不使用 iCloud 或 CloudKit 同步 MoodTracker 資料。

本機紀錄可能包含：

  • 紀錄標題與分類
  • 開始與結束時間、持續時間
  • 心情與能量評分
  • 您輸入的備註或感想
  • 維持資料一致性與同步所需的識別資訊

這些資料主要由 iOS App 本身在您的裝置上處理。 Gayu Dev 不會透過自有伺服器接收這些紀錄。

03

Google Calendar 整合

Google Calendar 為選用功能。只有在您主動選擇連接 Google 帳號並授權後， MoodTracker 才會存取 Google Calendar。

我們要求的 Google Calendar 權限

MoodTracker 目前要求以下 Google Calendar 權限：

  • calendar.app.created： 用於管理由 MoodTracker 建立的子日曆與其中的事件。
  • calendar.calendarlist.readonly： 用於唯讀查看您的 Calendar List，以尋找 MoodTracker 建立的子日曆。

MoodTracker 的設計目標是管理由 App 建立的 MoodTracker 子日曆， 而不是修改您原本的其他 Google 日曆。

同步到 Google Calendar 的內容

當您啟用 Google Calendar 同步時，MoodTracker 可能將以下資訊傳送至 Google Calendar：

  • 分類與標題
  • 開始時間與結束時間
  • 心情評分
  • 能量評分
  • 您輸入的備註或感想
  • 用於對應 MoodTracker 紀錄的識別碼
  • App 用於呈現事件的顏色或同步設定

這些資料是為了讓您在 Google Calendar 中查看與管理 MoodTracker 紀錄。資料會直接由您的裝置傳送至 Google API； Gayu Dev 不經營接收或儲存這些內容的伺服器。

本機保存的 Google 同步資訊

為了維持同步一致性，MoodTracker 可能在您的裝置上保存有限的 Google Calendar 同步資訊，例如：

  • Calendar ID 與 Event ID
  • Google 帳號 Email 與 Google 使用者識別碼
  • 紀錄與 Google Event 的對應資訊
  • 用於判斷內容是否需要重新同步的同步指紋

部分 Calendar 帳號與同步 metadata 會使用 iOS 的資料保護機制保存在裝置上。

Google 登入與憑證

MoodTracker 使用 Google Sign-In SDK 進行登入與授權。 Google Sign-In 會使用 iOS Keychain 保存先前的登入狀態與相關憑證。 Gayu Dev 沒有自己的伺服器接收或保存您的 Google OAuth access token 或 refresh token。

Google User Data 與 Limited Use

MoodTracker 僅將從 Google Workspace API 取得的資訊用於提供您主動使用的 Google Calendar 同步功能。我們不會將 Google 使用者資料出售給廣告商、 data broker，或用於廣告投放。

MoodTracker 對透過 Google Workspace API 取得資訊的使用， 將遵循 Google API Services User Data Policy ，包括 Limited Use 要求。

當資料傳送至 Google Calendar 後，Google 會依照其服務條款、 隱私政策與您的 Google 帳號設定處理該資料。

04

CSV 匯入與匯出

MoodTracker 支援 CSV 匯入與匯出。您自行選擇要匯入的檔案， 以及匯出檔案的儲存或分享位置。

Gayu Dev 不會透過自有伺服器接收您的 CSV 檔案。 如果您自行將匯出的檔案分享至第三方服務， 該服務對資料的處理將受其自身條款與隱私政策規範。

05

Widget、Live Activity 與裝置端功能

MoodTracker 目前可使用 Widget 與 Live Activity。 這些功能使用在裝置上處理的資訊，MoodTracker 不會因此將您的紀錄傳送至 Gayu Dev 的伺服器。

未來版本可能加入提醒、週報或月報等裝置端功能。 如果這些功能仍完全在裝置上處理，您的 MoodTracker 紀錄不會因此傳送給 Gayu Dev。 若未來功能需要新的伺服器、遠端推播或其他會實質改變資料處理方式的服務， 我們會更新本政策與相關 App 隱私揭露。

06

分析、廣告與追蹤

目前版本的 MoodTracker：

  • 不使用第三方分析 SDK（例如 Firebase Analytics、Mixpanel 或 Amplitude）
  • 不使用第三方 Crashlytics 或 Sentry 類型的 crash reporting SDK
  • 不包含廣告 SDK
  • 不使用 IDFA
  • 不進行跨 App 或跨網站追蹤
  • 不出售或出租您的個人資料

App 內的統計與分析功能是針對您的 MoodTracker 紀錄在裝置上進行， 不是將使用資料送往 Gayu Dev 的外部分析服務。

07

當您主動聯絡我們

如果您主動透過 Email 聯絡 Gayu Dev，我們可能收到您主動提供的資訊，例如：

  • 您的 Email 地址
  • 問題描述或回饋
  • 您主動附上的截圖
  • 您主動附上的匯出檔案或其他附件

我們只會將這些資訊用於回覆與處理您的支援需求， 並僅在處理支援、保留必要紀錄或遵循適用法律義務所合理需要的期間內保存。

08

您的選擇與資料控制

您可以選擇：

  • 不連接 Google Calendar，仍使用 MoodTracker 的本機功能
  • 刪除單筆 MoodTracker 紀錄
  • 清除 App 內的本機資料
  • 登出 Google
  • 撤銷 MoodTracker 的 Google OAuth 授權
  • 刪除 MoodTracker 建立的 Google 子日曆與相關事件
  • 在 Google 帳號設定中管理第三方 App 存取權

請注意： 單純登出 Google 或撤銷 OAuth 授權，不等同於刪除已經同步到 Google Calendar 的資料。若您希望刪除 Google 端的 MoodTracker 子日曆或事件， 請使用 App 中提供的刪除功能，或直接在 Google Calendar 中管理相關資料。

09

資料分享與第三方服務

Gayu Dev 不會出售、出租或提供您的 MoodTracker 紀錄給廣告商或 data broker。

當您主動啟用 Google Calendar 時，為了提供您要求的同步功能， 指定的 MoodTracker 資料會傳送給 Google。 除此之外，目前沒有由 Gayu Dev 經營的後端服務接收您的 MoodTracker 紀錄。

10

資料安全

MoodTracker 採取與目前架構相符的安全措施，包括使用 iOS 的本機資料保護機制、 Google Sign-In 提供的驗證機制，以及透過 HTTPS 與 Google API 通訊。

不過，任何儲存或傳輸方式都無法保證絕對安全。 我們會持續以合理方式改善 App 的資料保護與安全性。

11

兒童隱私

MoodTracker 是一般用途的生活紀錄 App，並非特別設計或導向 13 歲以下兒童。 如果我們得知透過支援管道取得了不應保留的兒童個人資訊， 我們會採取合理措施處理或刪除該資訊。

12

政策更新

如果 MoodTracker 未來新增會實質改變資料存取、傳輸、分享或保存方式的功能， 我們會更新本隱私權政策，並調整 App Store 或其他平台上的相關隱私揭露。

頁面上方的「最後更新日期」會顯示本政策最近一次更新時間。

MoodTracker Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Gayu Dev (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use MoodTracker.

In short: your primary MoodTracker records are stored on your device. Gayu Dev does not operate its own server, backend, or database that receives your MoodTracker records. If you choose to enable Google Calendar integration, selected record information is sent directly from your device to Google to provide the synchronization feature you requested.

02

Information stored on your device

MoodTracker uses on-device storage for records you create. The current version does not use iCloud or CloudKit to synchronize MoodTracker records.

Local records may include:

  • record titles and categories;
  • start time, end time, and duration;
  • mood and energy ratings;
  • notes or reflections you enter; and
  • identifiers needed to maintain record consistency and synchronization.

These records are primarily processed on your device. Gayu Dev does not receive them through a Gayu Dev-operated server.

03

Google Calendar integration

Google Calendar is optional. MoodTracker accesses Google Calendar only after you choose to connect a Google Account and grant permission.

Google Calendar permissions

MoodTracker currently requests:

  • calendar.app.created — to manage the secondary calendar and events created by MoodTracker.
  • calendar.calendarlist.readonly — to read your Calendar List in order to locate the secondary calendar created by MoodTracker.

MoodTracker is designed to manage the MoodTracker secondary calendar created by the app, rather than modify your existing unrelated calendars.

Information synchronized to Google Calendar

If you enable Google Calendar synchronization, MoodTracker may send the following information to Google Calendar:

  • category and title;
  • start and end times;
  • mood rating;
  • energy rating;
  • notes or reflections you entered;
  • an identifier used to associate the Google event with a MoodTracker record; and
  • app-defined event color or synchronization settings.

This information is sent so you can view and manage your MoodTracker records in Google Calendar. It is transmitted directly from your device to Google APIs. Gayu Dev does not operate a server that receives or stores this synchronized content.

Google synchronization metadata stored locally

To maintain synchronization, MoodTracker may store limited metadata locally on your device, including:

  • calendar and event identifiers;
  • your Google Account email address and Google user identifier;
  • record-to-event association information; and
  • a synchronization fingerprint used to determine whether a record needs to be updated.

Certain calendar account and synchronization metadata is stored on-device using iOS data protection.

Google Sign-In and credentials

MoodTracker uses the Google Sign-In SDK for authentication and authorization. Google Sign-In uses the iOS Keychain to preserve previous sign-in state and related credentials. Gayu Dev does not operate a server that receives or stores your Google OAuth access token or refresh token.

Google User Data and Limited Use

Information obtained through Google Workspace APIs is used only to provide the Google Calendar functionality that you choose to use. We do not sell Google user data to advertisers or data brokers, and we do not use it for advertising.

MoodTracker’s use of information received from Google Workspace APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Once information is stored in Google Calendar, Google processes that information under its own terms, privacy policy, and your Google Account settings.

04

CSV import and export

MoodTracker supports CSV import and export. You choose the file you import and where an exported file is saved or shared.

Gayu Dev does not receive your CSV files through a Gayu Dev-operated server. If you choose to share an exported file with another service, that service will handle the file under its own terms and privacy practices.

05

Widgets, Live Activities, and device-based features

MoodTracker currently supports widgets and Live Activities. These features use information processed on your device and do not, by themselves, send your MoodTracker records to a Gayu Dev server.

Future versions may add device-based reminders or weekly and monthly summaries. If those features remain entirely on-device, they will not cause your MoodTracker records to be sent to Gayu Dev. If a future feature introduces a new server, remote push service, or other material change in data handling, we will update this Policy and the relevant App Store or platform privacy disclosures.

06

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The current version of MoodTracker:

  • does not use third-party analytics SDKs such as Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or Amplitude;
  • does not use third-party crash-reporting SDKs such as Crashlytics or Sentry;
  • does not include advertising SDKs;
  • does not use IDFA;
  • does not track you across apps or websites; and
  • does not sell or rent your personal information.

MoodTracker’s in-app statistics and analysis are performed for your own records and are not an external analytics service operated by Gayu Dev.

07

When you contact us

If you contact Gayu Dev by email, we may receive information you choose to provide, such as:

  • your email address;
  • your question, feedback, or issue description;
  • screenshots you choose to attach; and
  • exported files or other attachments you choose to send.

We use this information only to respond to and handle your support request, and retain it only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide support, maintain necessary records, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

08

Your choices and controls

You can choose to:

  • use MoodTracker without connecting Google Calendar;
  • delete individual MoodTracker records;
  • clear local data within the app;
  • sign out of Google;
  • revoke MoodTracker’s Google OAuth authorization;
  • delete the MoodTracker secondary Google Calendar and its related events; and
  • manage third-party access through your Google Account settings.

Please note: signing out of Google or revoking OAuth authorization does not automatically delete information that has already been synchronized to Google Calendar. If you want to delete the MoodTracker calendar or events stored in Google, use the deletion controls provided by MoodTracker where available or manage the data directly in Google Calendar.

09

Data sharing and third-party services

Gayu Dev does not sell, rent, or provide your MoodTracker records to advertisers or data brokers.

If you choose to enable Google Calendar, selected MoodTracker information is transmitted to Google solely to provide the synchronization feature you requested. Apart from this user-initiated integration, Gayu Dev does not currently operate a backend service that receives your MoodTracker records.

10

Security

MoodTracker uses security measures appropriate to its current architecture, including iOS on-device data protection, authentication provided through Google Sign-In, and HTTPS communication with Google APIs.

No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We will continue to take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the app.

11

Children’s privacy

MoodTracker is a general-purpose personal activity and reflection app and is not specifically directed to children under 13. If we learn that personal information from a child has been provided to us through a support channel in circumstances where it should not be retained, we will take reasonable steps to address or delete it.

12

Changes to this Policy

If future versions of MoodTracker materially change how information is accessed, transmitted, shared, or retained, we will update this Privacy Policy and the relevant App Store or platform privacy disclosures.

The “Last updated” date at the top of this page shows when this Policy was most recently revised.
